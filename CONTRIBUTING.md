# Contributing and disclosure boundaries

This repository is an information and inquiry surface for a private research
corpus. It is not a public capture repository.

## Welcome

- technically specific use-case questions;
- requests for evidence layers or file formats;
- parser and validation requirements;
- proposals for controlled research evaluation;
- corrections to public documentation.

## Do not post publicly

- raw captures or archives;
- vehicle lists or private corpus indexes;
- MAC addresses, payload dumps, tokens, or precise timestamps from a target;
- private laboratory paths or source maps;
- security-sensitive reproduction material;
- third-party personal or location information.

Use the structured research-interest form without attaching private evidence.
Any controlled transfer is discussed separately.
